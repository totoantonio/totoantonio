Features.

  • From Plan to Daily List. — import the plan from Excel, CSV or even a photo of the printed sheet. Each salesman opens the app and sees today's stores.
  • Visits You Can Trust. — every visit saves the location, the distance to the store, a selfie and the order photo. Far from the store? It's flagged.
  • Calls Count Too. — stores covered by telemarketing are logged as calls, with their own sales and missed-call counts.
  • Sales per Brand — salesmen enter sales per brand on each visit; you see each brand against its monthly target.
  • Collections — money collected on each visit, with the reason when nothing was collected.
  • Nothing Slips — missed visits, visits not in the plan, new stores found in the field and no-location visits are all flagged.
  • Reports — dashboard, calendar and day, week or month reports per salesman, with CSV and Print / PDF.
  • Works Without Signal — visits are saved on the phone and sent with their original time when the signal is back.
  • An App on Every Phone — scan a QR code, log in once, add it to the Home Screen. No app store needed.
  • Photo Proof — every selfie and order photo carries the store, salesman, time and the place where it was logged.
  • Reach It Anywhere — turn on the public address once; salesmen open it anywhere with mobile data, nothing to install.

Up and Running in 3 Steps.

  1. Install It. Put it on one office Mac or Windows computer and add your salesmen and stores.
  2. Import Your Plan. From Excel, CSV or a photo of the plan you already print.
  3. Send the Team Out. Salesmen scan a QR code, follow their daily list and log every visit. You watch the numbers come in.

Opening It the First Time.

On a Mac

  1. Open DailyHabitTracker-Mac-0.2.8-build-10.dmg and drag Daily Habit Tracker onto the Applications folder.
  2. Double-click it. The first time, macOS asks if you want to open an app downloaded from the internet; click Open. It's signed and checked by Apple.
  3. Your browser opens the app. Create the manager account, or tap Explore Demo.

On Windows

  1. Unzip DailyHabitTracker-Windows.zip and open the Daily Habit Tracker folder.
  2. Double-click Daily Habit Tracker.exe. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Your browser opens the app. Keep the small black window open — closing it quits the app.

Questions.

Do my salesmen need to install anything?

No. They scan a QR code, log in once in their phone's browser and add it to the Home Screen so it opens like an app.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Today's stores open without signal and visits are saved on the phone, then sent with their original time when the signal is back.

Can salesmen reach it outside the office?

Yes. Turn on the public address once in Connect Phones; salesmen then open it anywhere with mobile data, nothing to install.

Can you set it up for us?

Yes. Virtual Setup installs it on your office computer and your salesmen's phones over a video call. Coverage Plan Setup turns your existing plan into the app. Prefer someone at your office? On-Site Setup is ₱5,000 a day plus plane fare, hotel and per diem — we send a quote first.

Where is my data kept?

On your own computer. Visits, photos and reports are not stored on someone else's server.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your data stays. Paste an activation code in Settings → About and carry on where you left off.
