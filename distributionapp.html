Features.

  • Approvals that find you. — a soft chime when an order or a new customer needs your OK. Credit holds stop orders for customers with overdue invoices.
  • Sales — customers with credit terms, limits and discounts; orders with approval; invoices made for you; delivery steps from Packed to Delivered.
  • Collections — cash, bank transfer, GCash, Maya and checks. Post-dated checks wait until you mark them cleared.
  • Stock and buying — units and cases, average cost, suggested purchase orders, supplier price lists from PDF, Excel or CSV, and days of stock left.
  • Books that do themselves — Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance and General Ledger, worked out for you. Send your bookkeeper one CSV.
  • Money — receivables and payables by age, expenses, a 4-week cash forecast, tax estimates and payroll with payslips.
  • Your team — roles for Admin, Staff, Salesman and Delivery, permissions down to each report, and an activity log nobody can edit.
  • An app on every phone — add it to the Home Screen and it opens like a real app. Salesmen can write orders without signal.

Printouts with your company details.

  • A4 Sales Invoice
  • Sales Order
  • Delivery Receipt
  • Credit Memo
  • Statement of Account
  • Price List
  • Business Cards

Local payments, taxes and payroll.

  • Post-dated checks. — tracked until they clear, with bounced checks counted against credit.
  • GCash, Maya and bank — as payment methods, plus an optional Payment QR Code salesmen can show customers.
  • BIR taxes — VAT and percentage tax estimates and tax payments.
  • Withholding tax — 2307 deductions kept with each payment, with a reason.
  • Payroll — with SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG contributions.

Up and running in 3 steps.

  1. Install it. Put it on one office Mac or Windows computer and set up your company.
  2. Bring your team. Staff scan a QR code and sign in on their phones. No app store needed.
  3. Run your day. Orders, approvals, invoices, deliveries and payments flow through, and your books update themselves.

Questions.

Do I need internet?

No. The app runs on one office computer and staff connect over the office Wi-Fi. Work From Anywhere is optional for salesmen on the road; if the connection drops, the office keeps working and salesmen's phones send their orders once they are back online.

Do my staff need to install anything?

No. They scan a QR code and use the app in their phone's browser, and can add it to the Home Screen so it opens like an app.

Where is my data kept?

On your own computer, with daily backups. It is not stored on someone else's server.

Which computers does it run on?

Mac and Windows. Staff can use any phone, tablet or computer with a web browser.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your data stays. Paste an activation code in License and carry on where you left off.
