Features.

  • Every app, at once. — one EQ for the whole system, on speakers, AirPods and Bluetooth.
  • 10-band EQ — 32 Hz to 16 kHz, with a live curve and a live spectrum as you play.
  • Spatial sound — virtual 5-speaker surround, stereo width and crossfeed for headphones.
  • 13 presets — from Bass Boost to Podcast, plus your own. One tap brings it all back.
  • AutoEq profiles — import a correction curve made for your exact headphones.

And so much more.

  • Menu bar app
  • Extra Bass
  • Clarity
  • Enhanced stereo
  • Auto level
  • Volume boost to 200%
  • Left/right balance
  • Night mode
  • Ambience
  • Fidelity
  • Pitch shift
  • Export and import settings
  • Low CPU mode
  • Analyzer on or off

Ready in 3 steps.

  1. Turn it on. Open macEQ and switch on System EQ from the bottom bar.
  2. Shape your sound. Pick a preset, or drag the ten bands yourself.
  3. Keep it. Save it as your own preset and bring it back with one tap.
