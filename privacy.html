Privacy Policy

Effective August 18, 2026

macEQ, KeyboardS, and Upscape all run locally on your Mac. None of them collect personal data, require an account, or send anything to a server.

Applies to all apps

This policy covers every app published by totoantonio: macEQ, KeyboardS, and Upscape. None of these apps collect personal data, listening or browsing history, keystrokes, screen contents, analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking data. There are no accounts, no logins, and no third-party SDKs. Settings and presets are stored only on your Mac.

App-specific

macEQ

System audio access

macEQ requests Screen & System Audio Recording access so it can receive system audio from apps such as Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, browsers, media players, and other audio-producing apps.

This access is used only to apply real-time equalizer and audio effects on your Mac.

Local processing

macEQ processes audio locally on the device. macEQ does not record, save, store, upload, transmit, sell, or share captured audio.

macEQ does not capture screen contents. The macOS permission may appear under Screen & System Audio Recording because macOS groups system audio recording under that privacy area.

Network use

macEQ does not require an internet connection for equalizer, spatial, or effects processing.

Your control

You can enable or disable System EQ at any time from the macEQ window. Disabling System EQ restores normal system audio routing.

You can revoke macEQ's audio recording permission in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording.

App-specific

KeyboardS

No keystroke recording

KeyboardS is a menu bar reference for macOS keyboard shortcuts. It does not record keystrokes and does not request Input Monitoring or Accessibility access. It registers a single global hotkey (⌘ + K by default) to open its search window, and macOS handles that registration.

What it stores

Your chosen hotkey and preferences are saved on your Mac. Searches you type stay in memory while the window is open and are never logged or transmitted.

Network use

KeyboardS works fully offline. The shortcut library ships inside the app.

App-specific

Upscape

Your images stay on your Mac

Upscape upscales images on-device using Core ML and Apple's Neural Engine. Images are never uploaded to a server, sent to a cloud AI service, or shared with anyone. There is no image queue and no remote processing.

File access

Upscape only reads the images you open or drag into it, and only writes the upscaled files you choose to save. It does not scan your Photos library, folders, or drives on its own.

Network use

Upscape does not require an internet connection to upscale images.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above is updated. Continued use of the apps after a change means you accept the revised policy.
