Answers to common questions, and a direct line if you need a hand.

Frequently asked questions

  • Upscape says a model is “Not installed.” What should I do?

    Reinstall the app. Models are bundled with Upscape and should appear automatically after installation.

  • Why is export slow or failing on a large image?

    Try a lower output scale. Very large images are capped to help manage memory during processing and export.

  • Which model should I use?

    See the in-app model picker for guidance. It recommends models for portraits, illustrations, architecture, and general photos.

  • Are my images uploaded when I use Upscape?

    No. Upscape processes images locally on your Mac; your images are not uploaded to us.

  • What should I include when reporting a problem?

    Include your macOS version, Mac model, the model and output scale you used, and the steps that led to the issue.
