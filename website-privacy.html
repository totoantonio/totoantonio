Website Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This policy covers the totoantonio website and its support and checkout links. It does not replace the separate privacy policy for each app.

1. Consent and Your Choices

Google Analytics and Meta Pixel do not load unless you opt in to their separate categories in the privacy panel. Rejecting optional tracking does not limit the site. You can reopen the panel at any time using the Privacy settings button and withdraw either choice.

Your choices are stored in this browser's local storage. Clearing site data or using another browser resets them. Theme preference is also stored locally.

2. Analytics and Advertising Events

With Analytics consent, Google Analytics 4 receives page views, product-card selections (select_item), and clicks that begin checkout (begin_checkout). With Advertising consent, Meta Pixel receives page views, product interest (ViewContent), and checkout-start events (InitiateCheckout).

These events include the product name or ID, not names, email addresses, or support-message contents. The providers may receive browser and device details, IP address, referrer, and identifiers such as cookies, and may process them outside your country. See the Google Privacy Policy and Meta Privacy Policy for their processing and retention practices.

A checkout click indicates intent, not a completed purchase. This website does not send a purchase-confirmation event.

3. Google Fonts

Google Sans is served by Google Fonts so the site can display its typeface. This font request occurs even when optional analytics and advertising are declined and may expose connection details such as your IP address and browser information to Google.

4. Support Requests

If you send a support request, your name, email address, selected topic, optional macOS/device details, and message are sent through Formspree so we can receive and answer it. Formspree may process submissions in the United States or other countries. We do not send those form fields to Google Analytics or Meta Pixel, and we do not treat support requests as sales leads. Please do not include sensitive information. See the Formspree Privacy Policy.

5. Lemon Squeezy Checkout

Purchase links open Lemon Squeezy. Information you provide during checkout, including payment and billing details, is handled by Lemon Squeezy under its Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment credentials.

6. Privacy Requests

To ask about or request access to or deletion of information you submitted through a support form, contact us through Support or Upscape Support.
